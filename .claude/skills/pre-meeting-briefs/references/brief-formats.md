# Brief Formats — Section Sets by Meeting Type

This file defines which sections are included, their order, and any format-specific
instructions for each brief type. Claude reads this during Step 5 to select the right structure.

---

## Format: FIRST_CONTACT_BRIEF

**When:** Stage 0–1, no prior Attio/email history, cold intro or new referral.
**Badge colour:** Amber `#E6A817`
**Tone:** Curious, confident, not salesy. This is a listening meeting.
**Priority:** Make them feel understood before you make a single ask.

**Sections (in order):**
1. Quick Situational Strip
2. Who Are They (full power map if multiple attendees)
3. The Company (include sector macro brief — 2–3 paras on the industry moment)
4. First-Meeting Playbook ← ADAPTIVE SECTION
5. Talking Points (skew toward open questions, < 2 statements)
6. Watch-Outs
7. Desired Outcome

**First-Meeting Playbook content:**
- How to frame the opening (not a pitch — a single orienting statement that signals you've done your homework)
- The 1 question that matters most for this meeting (derived from the angle)
- What NOT to do: list 2–3 specific traps for this counterparty based on their profile
  (e.g. don't pitch the full platform to a operator-type CEO, don't name-drop to someone who values discretion)
No minute-by-minute agenda. No scripted sequence. Just the frame and the one key question.

**Note on Section 3 (Our History):**
For FIRST_CONTACT, history will likely be sparse or absent. State it plainly:
"First meeting — no prior engagement found." Note how the intro was made and any mutual
connections that could warm the opening. Do not pad this section.

## Format: DISCOVERY_BRIEF

**When:** Stage 1–2, qualified interest confirmed, scoping the mandate.
**Badge colour:** Teal `#1D9E75`
**Tone:** Thoughtful, exploratory. You're building the map, not navigating it yet.
**Priority:** Understand their world deeply enough to tailor the pitch.

**Sections (in order):**
1. Quick Situational Strip
2. Who Are They
3. The Company (include: what are they likely trying to achieve in the next 12 months?)
4. Our History
5. Open Commitments & Tasks
6. Deal & Opportunity Map
7. Talking Points (mix of questions and statements — 50/50)
8. Watch-Outs
9. Desired Outcome

**Discovery-specific guidance:**
- TP1 must be an open question that surfaces their priority — not a statement about BugleRock.
- Include a "What we don't know yet" block (3 bullets max): the specific gaps this meeting must fill to enable a proposal. Make them specific to this counterparty, not generic.
- Desired outcome: walk away with enough to write a proposal.

## Format: PITCH_BRIEF

**When:** Stage 2–3, proposal or IC note shared or about to be shared.
**Badge colour:** Berry `#912F63`
**Tone:** Confident, specific, data-grounded. You're making a case.
**Priority:** Land the narrative. Handle the objections before they're raised.

**Sections (in order):**
1. Quick Situational Strip
2. Who Are They (power map critical — know who decides)
3. The Company (include competitive intelligence)
4. Sector Macro Brief ← ADAPTIVE SECTION (2–3 paras — set the context for the pitch)
5. Our History
6. Open Commitments & Tasks
7. Deal & Opportunity Map
8. Talking Points (lead with the angle, close with the CTA)
9. Watch-Outs
10. Desired Outcome

**Competitive intelligence content:**
- Who else might be pitching for this mandate? (named if identifiable)
- What will they offer vs. what we offer? — 2–3 differentiators, not a feature list
- What's their likely objection to us vs. the competitor?

**Sector macro brief content:**
- What is happening in this sector right now that makes this mandate timely?
- 1 data point or recent deal that supports the pitch narrative
- The market context that makes our approach the right one for this moment

**Pitch-specific notes:**
- Sector macro and competitive intelligence are the only sections that won't appear in other brief types — everything else must add new information, not restate the Strip or Section 1–3 content.
- TP1 deploys the angle (already stated in the Strip — so the talking point is the *delivery vehicle*, not a restatement of what the angle is).
- Last talking point is the specific mandate ask.

## Format: DILIGENCE_BRIEF

**When:** Stage 3–4, term sheet shared, active data room or due diligence underway.
**Badge colour:** Deep Plum `#3E3452`
**Tone:** Precise, responsive, calm under pressure. Numbers and facts only.
**Priority:** Defend positions, manage the process, don't give ground unnecessarily.

**Sections (in order):**
1. Quick Situational Strip
2. Who Are They (note: diligence meetings often bring new counterparty advisors — ID them)
3. The Company
4. Our History (include: what's been shared in the data room, what they've reviewed)
5. Open Commitments & Tasks
6. Term Sheet / Open Points Summary ← ADAPTIVE SECTION
7. Deal & Opportunity Map
8. Talking Points (factual, specific, documented)
9. Watch-Outs
10. Desired Outcome

**Term sheet / open points summary content:**
Pull from Drive (term sheet, NDA, proposal) and email/Attio notes.
List all open points in a table:

| Point | Our position | Their position | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fee structure | 2% + 20% carried | Wants 1.5% flat | Open |
| Exclusivity | 90 days | Wants 60 days | Open |
| ...

Flag any point that could be a dealbreaker.

## Format: NEGOTIATION_BRIEF / CLOSE_BRIEF

**When:** Stage 4–5, terms being finalised, mandate about to sign.
**Badge colour:** Red `#D94F3D` (Negotiation) / Deep Plum (Close)
**Tone:** Measured, closing-oriented. Know your walk-aways.
**Priority:** Close the deal. Eliminate the final blocker.

**Sections (in order):**
1. Quick Situational Strip (prominently: WHAT IS THE REMAINING BLOCKER?)
2. Who Are They (decision authority — who actually signs?)
3. Our History
4. Open Commitments & Tasks
5. Term Sheet / Open Points Summary ← ADAPTIVE SECTION (condensed to unresolved items only)
6. Talking Points (all points lead to resolution — no discovery, no pitching)
7. Watch-Outs
8. Desired Outcome (PRIMARY must be: mandate signed / specific document executed)

**Negotiation-specific guidance:**
- State BATNA clearly in the brief (Best Alternative To Negotiated Agreement)
  — what is our walk-away point? (for internal use only, not in email delivery)
- Know their BATNA too if inferrable — do they have a competing offer?
- Every talking point should move toward closure, not re-open resolved points

## Format: RELATIONSHIP_BRIEF

**When:** No active deal, periodic check-in, "coffee", "catch up", LTR nurture.
**Badge colour:** Lavender `#A795AE`
**Tone:** Warm, curious, low-pressure. But never aimless — always have an agenda.
**Priority:** Deepen trust. Surface new needs. Plant seeds for the next mandate.

**Sections (in order):**
1. Quick Situational Strip
2. Who Are They (include the human detail — what do they care about beyond work?)
3. The Company (current state — what has changed since we last spoke?)
4. Our History (full timeline — this is a long-game meeting)
5. Open Commitments & Tasks
6. Referral & Network Map ← ADAPTIVE SECTION
7. Talking Points (lead with them, not us — 1st point is always about their world)
8. Watch-Outs
9. Desired Outcome (can be softer: "deepen relationship, surface one new need")

**Referral & network map content:**
- Who in their network could benefit from a BugleRock introduction?
- Which of our other clients or contacts would they benefit from knowing?
- Is there a co-investment or co-advisory angle?
- What event, publication, or introduction could we offer them as a value-add?

**Relationship-specific guidance:**
The hidden agenda of every relationship meeting: find the next deal thread.
But surface it naturally, never push it. The rule: 70% of the conversation should be
about their world. The 30% that's about BugleRock should be reactive (answering their
questions) or additive (offering something useful). Never explicitly pitch.

## Format: PORTFOLIO_REVIEW_BRIEF

**When:** Existing client, post-mandate, update or reporting meeting.
**Badge colour:** Muted Purple `#6D5479`
**Tone:** Accountable, forward-looking, value-demonstrating.
**Priority:** Show what we've done, validate the decision to work with us, surface the next mandate.

**Sections (in order):**
1. Quick Situational Strip
2. Who Are They
3. Our History (comprehensive — this is the relationship story)
4. Open Commitments & Tasks (this is CRITICAL — any overdue items = risk to relationship)
5. Portfolio Review Talking Points ← ADAPTIVE SECTION
6. Deal & Opportunity Map (what's next for them — identify next mandate early)
7. Watch-Outs
8. Desired Outcome

**Portfolio review talking points content:**
- What we delivered since the last meeting (specific, measurable)
- What is in progress (milestones, timeline)
- What comes next (proactively frame the roadmap)
- One value-add we've done that they may not know about (insight, intro, market intelligence)
- The question that opens the next mandate conversation:
  "Given where you are now, the next thing we'd love to explore with you is [X] —
  would it be useful to talk through that today?"

## Format: PARTNER_INTRO_BRIEF

**When:** Co-investor, co-advisor, intermediary, referral source.
**Badge colour:** Muted Purple `#6D5479`
**Tone:** Peer-to-peer, collaborative. Not a pitch — an exploration.
**Priority:** Assess alignment. Map the deal flow overlap. Define the working relationship.

**Sections (in order):**
1. Quick Situational Strip
2. Who Are They
3. The Company / Organisation
4. Our History (or lack thereof)
5. Referral & Network Map ← ADAPTIVE SECTION (how do our deal flows intersect?)
6. Talking Points (mutual benefit framing — "what we can do for each other")
7. Watch-Outs
8. Desired Outcome

---

## Format: INVESTOR_MEETING_BRIEF

**When:** BugleRock is selling — LP, family office, institutional investor meeting.
**Badge colour:** Berry `#912F63`
**Tone:** Confident, institutional, data-grounded.
**Priority:** Articulate BugleRock's differentiated value proposition. Advance the LP relationship.

**Sections (in order):**
1. Quick Situational Strip
2. Who Are They (LP profile — AUM, investment mandate, typical commitment size, existing managers)
3. The Institution (fund type, geography, investment philosophy)
4. Our History
5. Open Commitments & Tasks
6. LP Narrative ← ADAPTIVE SECTION
7. Talking Points
8. Watch-Outs
9. Desired Outcome

**LP narrative content:**
- BugleRock's positioning for this specific LP (what about us matches their mandate?)
- BRCS strategy overview tailored to their investment style
- Track record proof points relevant to this LP's focus
- The specific ask (commitment size / fund vehicle / next step)

---

## Format: RAPID_BRIEF

**When:** Meeting in < 30 minutes, or explicit "quick brief" request.
**Badge colour:** Orange `#E07B39` with ⚡ prefix
**Tone:** Urgent. Dense. No padding whatsoever.

**Sections (in order — maximum 4 sections):**
1. Quick Situational Strip (expanded — 2x the normal density)
2. Who Are They (name, title, company, 2-sentence arc only)
3. Talking Points (3 points max, no labels, just the content)
4. Watch-Outs (2 items max)

Note at top: "⚡ RAPID BRIEF — generated in < 10 minutes. Full brief available if needed."
Note at bottom: list any data sources that couldn't be fully fetched due to time constraint.
